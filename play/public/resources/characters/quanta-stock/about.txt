Eight-way sheets (8 x 9 frames of 32 x 32) made from the Pipoya stock characters in ../pipoya.
The four original facings and their walk are the Pipoya frames; the three-quarter facings and the seated row were
drawn from them for Quanta (~/.local/lib/quanta/wokas-stock on the dev host).
